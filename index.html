Rotary International District 4195

Service, leadership, and connection across southeastern Mexico.

Rotary District 4195 brings together clubs across Campeche, Chiapas, Oaxaca, Quintana Roo, Tabasco, Yucatán, and part of Veracruz. This homepage works as the public overview, while the inner pages carry denser district information.
